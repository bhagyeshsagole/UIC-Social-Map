## Apple design audit (A18)

> [!info] What this is
> An audit of the current frontend against the `apple-design` skill from [emilkowalski/skills](https://github.com/emilkowalski/skills) (MIT, commit `e8a175d`, 2026-10-02), vendored at [Skills/emil-kowalski-skills](Skills/emil-kowalski-skills/skills/apple-design/SKILL.md). Audited 2026-10-05. No source code changed. The fixes listed are **recommendations, not accepted decisions**.

**Scope:** `website/frontend/src/` as of A16 (map, place pins, discovery sheet, place details, Live events, building preview and venue sheet, date picker, Spotlight, create/profile dialogs, toast, map controls). The A17 files (`PrivateEventCreator.jsx`, `ShareEvent.jsx`, map legend, yellow private highlights) were being edited in parallel by Codex, so they were checked only through shared CSS.

**Method:** read every section of the skill. Checked each rule against `styles.css`, `Map.jsx`, `main.jsx`, `CampusEventList.jsx`, `LiveEvents.jsx` and `Spotlight.jsx`, plus headless-browser screenshots from the A16 checks.

Status key: **Pass** · **Partial** · **Gap** · **N/A**

### Findings by skill section

| # | Skill section | Status | What we found | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Response: feedback on press, no latency | Partial | `touch-action: manipulation` is set on buttons and links, so there's no 300 ms tap delay. Building hover preview and click have no added delay. Only `.uic-home` has an `:active` press state (`scale(.96)`). `-webkit-tap-highlight-color: transparent` removes the default touch flash, so most buttons give no press feedback on touch. | Add a shared `:active` press state (about `scale(.97)`, 100 ms) to buttons, cards, chips and nav items. |
| 2 | Direct manipulation: 1:1 tracking | Partial | Map panning tracks 1:1: mouse drag, custom two-finger trackpad pan (`panBy`, duration 0) and two-finger touch (`jumpTo`). On phones the discovery, detail and Live events sheets can't be dragged; they only open and close with buttons. | Make the phone sheet draggable (grab handle, pointer capture, respect grab offset), or use a bottom-sheet library such as Vaul. |
| 3 | Interruptibility | Partial | MapLibre `flyTo`/`easeTo` camera moves stop as soon as the user drags, so camera motion is interruptible. Sheets, dialogs and the date picker mount and unmount instantly with no motion, so there's nothing to interrupt but also no continuity. CSS transitions are only used for hover and colour, which is acceptable. | When sheet motion is added, use springs that start from the current on-screen value, not CSS keyframes. |
| 4 | Behaviour over animation: springs | Gap | No springs anywhere. Camera moves use fixed durations (`flyTo` 900–1000 ms, `easeTo` 450 ms for 2D/3D and north-up). | Use critically damped springs (damping 1.0, response 0.3–0.4) for sheets and popovers. Keep bounce for flicks only. |
| 5 | Velocity handoff | Gap | Built-in MapLibre drag pan carries momentum, but the custom two-finger touch handler resets `dragPan` on release, so that gesture stops dead. No sheet gestures exist to hand velocity to. | Pass release velocity into the camera (or let MapLibre inertia finish) after the custom two-finger pan. Do the same for future sheet drags. |
| 6 | Momentum projection | Gap | No snap points exist, so there's nothing to project. | If the phone sheet becomes draggable, give it snap points (peek, half, full) and choose the target with Apple's projection, `current + (v/1000)·d/(1−d)` with `d ≈ 0.998`. |
| 7 | Spatial consistency: same path in and out, anchored origins | Partial | The date picker opens directly under its trigger. The building preview appears at the cursor, and clicking a building marks it and opens its sheet, which ties the sheet to the building. Discovery and Live events sheets appear bottom-centre no matter which top nav item opened them, with no enter or exit path. | Give sheets one enter/exit path (rise from and return to the bottom). Set `transform-origin` on popovers (date picker, hours) to their trigger. |
| 8 | Hint in the direction of the gesture | N/A | No gesture-driven transitions yet. | Revisit with draggable sheets. |
| 9 | Rubber-banding at boundaries | Gap | Pitch (0–80°) and zoom (1–19) stop hard at their limits. Sheet scroll areas use `overscroll-behavior: contain`, which also stops hard. | Optional: soft resistance near camera limits. Native scroll bounce already covers lists on iOS. |
| 10 | Gesture details: tap targets, hysteresis, parallel recognition | Partial | MapLibre click tolerance (3 px default) separates a building click from a drag. Several targets are below 44 px: desktop nav items 33 px tall, icon buttons about 32 px, panel close 34 px, date-clear 32 px, date-picker day cells about 36 px. Place names on pins only show on hover, focus or selection, so touch users can't see names without tapping. | Grow touch targets to at least 44 px with padding (more on phones). Show labels for nearby or important pins on touch devices. |
| 11 | Frame-level smoothness | Partial | Transitions animate compositor-friendly properties (`transform`, `opacity`, `scale`) plus `background` and `border-color`. No `will-change` hints. Heavy `backdrop-filter` (blur 18–32 px) on many layers over a live WebGL map is a repaint cost. | Use fewer stacked blur layers (see §12). Add `will-change: transform` only to surfaces about to animate. |
| 12 | Materials and depth | Partial | Glass is used consistently: translucent background, `backdrop-filter` blur and saturate, bright top inset edge, day and night variants. Modal dialogs dim the background (`::backdrop` at `#0f172966` with 7 px blur; Spotlight at 3 px). Discovery sheets have no scrim, which is right for parallel panels. Gaps: glass sits on glass (`hours-pop glass` and the date picker inside the glass sheet; translucent cards inside Live events). Inside the sheet, the nested blur doesn't apply, which forced the A16 date picker to be nearly opaque. Blur doesn't grow with surface size: the big sheet uses 24 px while the small preview uses 26 px, the date picker 30 px and the nav pill 28 px. Sections are split by 1 px borders (`list-toolbar`, `event-scope-tags`, venue sheet results). Surfaces appear and disappear instantly instead of materialising. | Make nested surfaces solid or tinted rather than glass. Scale blur and shadow with surface size. Replace 1 px dividers under sticky areas with a soft fade. Animate blur and scale together when a sheet appears. |
| 13 | Multimodal feedback | N/A | No sound or haptics, which suits a web map. Visual feedback exists: toast for saves, feed status, map-unavailable state. | Optional: Vibration API only for key commits on phones. |
| 14 | Reduced motion and accessibility | Partial | `prefers-reduced-motion` turns off all CSS transitions and animations. It doesn't cover JavaScript camera flights (`flyTo` 900–1000 ms swoops), the largest motion in the app. No handling for `prefers-reduced-transparency` or `prefers-contrast`. Day/night switches instantly at sunset via `setStyle`, an abrupt brightness jump. | Under reduced motion, use `jumpTo` or a short `easeTo` instead of `flyTo`. Add solid fallbacks for reduced transparency and stronger borders for more contrast. Cross-fade the theme change. |
| 15 | Typography: tracking, leading, sizing | Partial | Headings use negative tracking (−0.9 to −1.5 px) and small caps labels use positive tracking (1–1.8 px), as the skill advises. Leading is 1.35–1.7 throughout. Gaps: tracking is in px, not em, so it doesn't scale with size. Custom faces (DM Sans, Manrope from Google Fonts) are used instead of the system font. | Set a minimum of about 11 px (12+ on glass). Convert sizes and tracking to `rem`/`em`. Keep the custom faces if they're a brand choice, and note it. |

Pass. **Familiarity / Wayfinding:** close button top-right on every sheet, Escape closes, active nav state, sheet titles, "Back to events". Pass. **Specific labels:** Explore, Live events, My Events, Saved. Pass. **Feedback kinds:** completion (toast) and status/warning (feed status) exist; inline validation relies on native `required`. Partial. **Agency:** deleting an event draft removes it immediately with no undo. The theme follows the sun with no manual light/dark choice. Gap. **Craft:** colours adapt to light and dark. The nested-glass and small-text issues above weaken it. Partial. | Add undo to draft deletion (toast with Undo). Add a light/dark/auto choice. Check inline validation in the create flow. |
