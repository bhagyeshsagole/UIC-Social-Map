<!-- id: art-institute -->
## Art Institute of Chicago
Spend an afternoon with paintings, sculpture, and photography. Bring your UIC i-card for free general admission.

<!-- id: busy-burger -->
## Busy Burger
Burgers, hand-cut fries, and shakes on Taylor Street. An easy stop between campus and Little Italy.

<!-- id: two-shades -->
## Two Shades Cafe
Coffee and a slow morning on Taylor Street. Try a latte, or grab a cold brew on your way to campus.

<!-- id: gallery-400 -->
## Gallery 400
A free contemporary art gallery right by campus. Drop in for an exhibition or check the calendar for artist talks.

<!-- id: first-ascent -->
## First Ascent · Block 37
Bouldering above the Loop, with yoga and space to work out. Discounted memberships are available to full-time students.

<!-- id: three-run-two -->
## 3RUN2 · University Village
Meet other runners near Halsted and Maxwell on Tuesday evenings. Come ready to run; there is no bag drop.

<!-- id: cairo-kebab -->
## Cairo Kebab
Egyptian and Mediterranean food on Maxwell Street, with halal meat and vegetarian and vegan options.

<!-- id: ml-kitchen -->
## ML Kitchen
Chinese takeout on Taylor Street, known for salt-and-pepper wings. Order online for the student discount.

<!-- id: de-pasada -->
## De Pasada
Tacos, burritos, and tortas a short walk west on Taylor. Vegetarian versions come with beans, cheese, and guacamole.

<!-- id: azul-18 -->
## Azul 18 on Taylor
Latin-inspired brunch with chilaquiles, Benedicts, and crepes. Open mornings until 3pm, so plan it before an afternoon class.

<!-- id: jims-original -->
## Jim's Original
The Maxwell Street Polish stand next to campus, open from early morning to late night. Show your UIC ID for a free soda.

<!-- id: primos -->
## Primo's Chicago Pizza
Pizza, sandwiches, and pasta in Greektown, a few blocks north of campus. The student discount also covers catering for club events.

<!-- id: ghareeb-nawaz -->
## Ghareeb Nawaz
Halal Pakistani and Indian food on Roosevelt. Big portions, and a $7 student meal deal before 4pm.

<!-- id: als-beef -->
## Al's #1 Italian Beef
The original Taylor Street Italian beef stand. Order it dipped, with sweet or hot peppers.

<!-- id: jenis -->
## Jeni's Splendid Ice Creams
Scoops in Fulton Market, open past midnight. Dairy-free and gluten-free flavors are on the board.

<!-- id: hull-house -->
## Jane Addams Hull-House Museum
The original settlement house, right on campus. Free, and a quick stop between classes.

<!-- id: cultural-center -->
## Chicago Cultural Center
See the world's largest Tiffany glass dome, then catch a free concert, film, or exhibition.

<!-- id: design-museum -->
## Design Museum of Chicago
Free exhibitions on graphic design, architecture, and how things are made, across from Millennium Park.

<!-- id: mocp -->
## Museum of Contemporary Photography
A free photography museum at Columbia College, a short Blue Line or bus ride from campus.

<!-- id: nmma -->
## National Museum of Mexican Art
Mexican, Latino, and Chicano art in Pilsen, always free. Its Day of the Dead exhibition is a fall favorite.

<!-- id: mca -->
## Museum of Contemporary Art
One of the largest contemporary art museums in the country. Illinois residents get in free on Tuesdays, when it stays open late.

<!-- id: gamespace -->
## Chicago Gamespace
A video game museum where you can play the collection. Free with your UIC i-card on Sunday afternoons.

<!-- id: uic-rec-srf -->
## UIC Student Recreation Facility
The east-campus gym, pool, and climbing wall. Your membership is already in your student fees.

<!-- id: uic-rec-sfc -->
## UIC Sport & Fitness Center
The west-campus gym and pool by Student Center West. Same student membership as the SRF.

<!-- id: lincoln-park-zoo -->
## Lincoln Park Zoo
A free zoo open every day of the year. Good for a weekend walk with friends.

<!-- id: chipotle -->
## Chipotle · Clinton & Roosevelt
Burritos and bowls a few blocks east of campus. Verify your student status in Chipotle Rewards for bonus points.

<!-- id: papa-johns -->
## Papa John's · Taylor Street
Pizza delivery and carryout on Taylor, open until 4am for late study nights.

<!-- id: taco-bell -->
## Taco Bell · Taylor Street
Late-night tacos on Taylor Street. The vegetarian menu is certified by the American Vegetarian Association.

<!-- id: portillos -->
## Portillo's · Canal & Taylor
Chicago-style hot dogs and Italian beef at Canal and Taylor, a short ride east of campus.

<!-- id: starbucks-uv -->
## Starbucks · University Village
The Halsted Street Starbucks, managed by UIC business students.

<!-- id: subway-uv -->
## Subway · University Village
Quick subs on Halsted in University Village, just south of campus.
